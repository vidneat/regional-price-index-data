# Regional Price Index: Multi-Channel Digital Subscription Prices by Country

Release **2026.11** (snapshot `2026.11.01`, data status `live`).

Local list prices of 25 digital subscriptions, games and devices (37 plan tiers) across 18 storefronts and four checkout channels (iOS App Store, Google Play, direct web, Steam), with FX-converted USD, tax- and gift-card-adjusted landed cost, IMF PPP and work-hour affordability, payment friction and a four-tier verification flag. Every row was observed on an official storefront or vendor page within 30 days of the snapshot; storefronts not yet observed are omitted, never estimated. Snapshot 2026.11.01.

## Files

- `prices.csv`: 618 rows
- `countries.csv`: 18 storefronts
- `history.csv`: 618 price points
- `prices.json`, `datapackage.json`, `.zenodo.json`, `dataset-metadata.json`

Every row was observed on an official storefront or vendor page; routes not yet observed are omitted.
